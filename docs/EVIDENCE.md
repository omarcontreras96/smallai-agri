# Evidence pack — does the price band work?

Numbers below come from `models/metrics.json` (generated by `model/train.py`, Oct 3 2026) and the tests in `model/tests/`. Reproduce with the commands at the end.

## Headline
- **The band is calibrated where it matters.** We checked year by year (2018–2025), fitting each year only on earlier data. The 10–90% band caught the real price 81% of the time at 1–3 month gaps, 79% at 4–6 months and 77% at 7–12 months, against a target of 80%.
- **It beats "last price I heard".** On the held-out test period (Jan 2025 – Apr 2026), the middle estimate (p50) is off by 402 UGX/kg on average, against 467 for the last observed price: 14% smaller errors. The gain grows with staleness: +2% at 1 month, +16% at 7–12 months.
- **It beats what a spreadsheet would do.** The no-AI comparison is a spreadsheet band: last price × historical percentiles of price change. At 7–12 month gaps the model scores better on pinball loss (a standard accuracy score for bands) in 7 of 8 years. Its bands also catch the real price more often (77% vs 69%).
- **It says when it doesn't know.** Bands widen with data age, from 37% of p50 at a 1-month gap to 82% at 7–12 months. That width, together with the data age, drives the "not sure, ask the co-op / extension officer" reply.
- **It is small.** The farmer path needs `bands.json` (22 KB) plus a 5 KB pure-Python nowcaster: no GPU, no cloud, works offline. The three model files (250 KB each) only rebuild the bands, about once a month, on a laptop.

## What the model does
For each (crop, market) it forecasts the retail price for the current month and the next two, as a band (p10, p50, p90) in UGX/kg.

- **Input:** WFP monthly retail prices for maize and beans in 28 Ugandan towns (2008–2026).
- **Model:** LightGBM quantile regression on the log price ratio between the target month T and the market's last observed month L. The gap g = T − L (1–12 months) is a feature. Other features: the market's own lags (1, 2, 3, 12 months); last year's change over the same calendar window; distance from the market's 24-month mean; premium over the national mean; national momentum; calendar months; crop; market.
- **Why the gap matters:** town data stops in Apr 2026, and in Dec 2025 – Jan 2026 for most markets, but farmers sell today. So the current-month band is really a 6–10 month-ahead forecast, and it has to be honest about that.
- **Calibration:** raw quantile-GBM bands were too narrow in every year we tested (2016–2025; 54–69% coverage on average per gap group, vs the 80% target), so we never ship them. Per gap, we learned two corrections from rolling-origin out-of-sample predictions (train on years before Y, predict year Y):
  1. pull the median toward the last observed price;
  2. widen the band by conformal calibration (CQR) until about 80% of past prices fall inside.

  The learned pull is itself evidence. At a 1-month gap the shipped median is 90% "last price" and 10% model. From 9 months on it is 100% model. The model earns its place when the data is old, which is exactly Noor's situation.

## Nowcaster — updating a band from farmers' reports
`model/nowcast.py` updates a market's current-month band from offers that farmers report by SMS. It is a Bayesian update on log price, with the model band as the starting estimate and each report treated as a reading with 30% noise. Because offers come from the buyers the tool is meant to check, it has guards: one vote per phone; no change until 2 different phones report; reports far from the expected price count for less; reports fade over about a month.

Real Mbale beans band for Oct 2026 (p50 3,650 UGX/kg), synthetic offers:

| Offers reported (UGX/kg) | p50 after | 10–90 band after |
|---|---:|---|
| none | 3,650 | 2,790–4,840 |
| 3,200 / 3,150 / 3,300 (three phones) | 3,380 | 2,850–4,050 |
| 3,600 / 3,700 / 1,000 (one lowball) | 3,350 | 2,790–4,060 |
| 2,000 three times from one phone | unchanged | unchanged |
| 2,400 from five phones | 2,740 | 2,360–3,210 |

These are behaviour checks with synthetic offers (`model/tests/test_nowcast.py`), not an accuracy test: we have no real offer data yet.

## Data
### Source
**WFP Food Prices – Uganda**, Humanitarian Data Exchange: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/wfp-food-prices-for-uganda
- License: Creative Commons Attribution for Intergovernmental Organisations (**CC BY-IGO**).
- Downloaded Oct 3 2026. File `data/raw/wfp_food_prices_uga.csv`: 4.3 MB, 34,328 rows, 43 markets, 38 commodities, Jan 2006 – Aug 2026.
- **What we use** (`data/processed/monthly.csv`, 4,468 rows): retail, per kg, UGX; maize ("Maize (white)" from 2011, plus "Maize" 2010–2018 where no white-maize price exists) and beans; 29 town markets, 28 of them in the bands (Kiboga dropped, last observed 2014).
- No other dataset trains or feeds the model. Other sources we evaluated are listed in `docs/DATA.md`.

### What the data does NOT cover (and what we do about it)
| Gap | Effect | What the tool does |
|---|---|---|
| **Retail in towns, not farm-gate.** No farm-gate series for maize/beans after the wholesale series ended (May 2022). | A fair farm-gate offer sits below the retail band, so offers tend to read LOW. | Reply says the reference is town market retail; the farmer judges the gap (transport, margin). |
| **Town data ends Apr 2026.** Only 8 bean markets and 2 maize markets (Arua, Gulu) report after Jan 2026; most stop Dec 2025 – Jan 2026; Wakiso, Mukono, Tororo (and Owino maize) stop Apr 2025; Mbale maize stops May 2025. | Oct 2026 bands are 6–10 month forecasts (17–18 months for the oldest), 50–100% of p50 wide. | Width and data age drive the "not sure, ask a person" reply. Under the 6-month data-age rule, 10 of 56 bands give a verdict on Oct 4 2026; the rest fall back. The nowcaster narrows bands as reports arrive. |
| **Refugee-settlement markets excluded.** 13 settlements are the only markets reporting May–Aug 2026 (17,889 raw rows, all commodities). | Their prices reflect humanitarian supply chains, not the town markets where Noor sells. | Excluded by design. |
| **9 of 28 markets are in Karamoja** (Kaabong, Karenga, Karita, Kotido, Makaratin, Moroto, Nabilatuk, Namalu, Napak). | Food-deficit, mostly pastoral area: consumer prices, not producer areas. | Each market gets its own band, but Karamoja prices do enter the national-average features used for every market. |
| **No quality, variety or moisture grade.** One mid-month price per market and month. | Beans mix varieties; wet or poor-quality grain sells below the band. | Reply is a reference band, not a quote. The farmer makes the call. |
| **Persona's highlands are fictional.** Nearest real analogue: Mt Elgon coffee area (Mbale, Kapchorwa). | Mbale beans are current to Apr 2026, but Mbale maize is stale since May 2025. | Demo maize with Gulu or Arua; beans with Mbale. |
| **Price shocks** (e.g. 2017–2018). | Coverage fell to ~0.6 in 2018. | Fallback + nowcaster; we report the bad year, not just the average. |
| **Local unit sizes** (basin, tin, bag) are conventions, not measured. | A wrong kg conversion makes a wrong verdict. | The reply asks the farmer when a unit is ambiguous (`service/units.py`). |

### Honesty notes on the evaluation
- Hyperparameters were picked on the 2024 validation year (trained up to 2023) only.
- The calibration design (median pull + CQR) was chosen after seeing rolling-origin results that include 2025, so the 2025–26 test numbers are slightly optimistic. The year-by-year table is the fairer view.
- The nowcaster's 30% offer noise is an assumption, not estimated from data. Two coordinated lowball phones can still move p50 about 20%.
